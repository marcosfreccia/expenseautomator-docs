Getting started

Create your account ​

Choose how you want to register, confirm your account details, and verify your email before beginning the guided setup.

Before you register ​

ExpenseAutomator offers a free plan and Premium options. The registration page shows the selected plan, its price, and whether a trial applies before you submit any details.

A payment card is not required to create the free account or begin the Premium trial. Always review the current plan details shown on the registration page.

Register with email ​

Open the pricing page

Go to https://expenseautomator.com/pricing and select a plan.

Enter your account details

Provide your email address, create a password of at least eight characters, and enter the password again for confirmation.

Complete the security check

Complete the reCAPTCHA challenge, then submit the registration form.

Verify your email

Open the verification message sent to your inbox and follow its activation link. Expired or previously used links cannot activate an account.

Premium Plan Checkout

If you choose one of the Premium Monthly or Annual plans, you will be redirected to the Stripe payment flow.

Register with Google ​

Select Sign up with Google on the registration page and complete Google's authorisation flow. ExpenseAutomator preserves the plan selected before you leave the page.

If the verification message does not arrive ​

Check the spam or junk folder and confirm that the address entered during registration is correct. Avoid creating several accounts in quick succession; instead, return to sign in and follow the available account recovery guidance.
