Reports ​

ExpenseAutomator Reports help you understand how income, spending and retained money change over time.

Reports combine your imported transactions into a longer-term financial picture. You can compare months, identify your largest spending areas and follow how income was distributed across your expenses.

Report period ​

Use the period controls in the top right corner to choose how much history to include:

  • 6M: the latest six months
  • YTD: the current year to date
  • 1Y: the latest year
  • 2Y: the latest two years
  • 5Y: the latest five years
  • All: all available history

Free accounts can view six-month and year-to-date reports. The one-year, two-year, five-year and all-history periods require Premium.

INFO

Report totals are calculated from the transactions available in ExpenseAutomator. Import newer statements to keep reports up to date.

Transactions assigned to a transfer category are excluded so moving money between your own accounts does not count as income or spending.

Financial pulse ​

The Financial pulse section summarizes the selected period.

Kept after spending is your recorded income minus recorded spending. A positive result is displayed in green and a negative result in red.

The income retained percentage shows how much of your income remained after spending. The summary also displays:

  • Total income
  • Total spending
  • Average monthly spending
  • The number of months where income was greater than spending

WARNING

The amount kept after spending is not your bank balance. It reflects reportable transactions for the selected period and does not automatically include future bills or planned spending.

Surplus allocation ​

Surplus allocation separates money that remained after spending from money you deliberately moved into savings or investments.

It displays:

  • Operating surplus: income minus spending
  • Saved / invested: surplus linked to savings and investment movements
  • Unallocated surplus: operating surplus that has not been identified as saved or invested

When surplus has been allocated in more than one currency, the currency breakdown shows the amounts assigned to savings and investments.

To populate this section, link outgoing contributions to a savings or investment movement and mark them as saved surplus.

Money in motion ​

The Money in motion chart compares each month in the selected period.

  • Green bars represent income
  • Spending bars represent money spent
  • The line represents the net amount remaining after spending

Hover over the chart to see the income, spending and net value for a particular month. Months below the zero line finished with more spending than income.

Where life costs you ​

This section groups spending by its assigned category or budget.

The chart and list show the six largest spending areas, including:

  • The category or budget name
  • Its percentage of total reportable spending
  • The amount spent

Hover over a section of the chart or an item in the list to focus on that spending area.

Uncategorized spending appears as Uncategorized. Assign categories or budgets to your transactions to make this section more useful.

TIP

Review uncategorized transactions before using category reports to make spending decisions.

Signals worth noticing ​

ExpenseAutomator highlights three patterns from the selected period:

  • The month that left the most money after spending
  • Your largest spending category
  • The month with the highest spending

These signals provide a quick starting point for reviewing the report. Click Explore the transactions behind this report to open the Transactions page and investigate the underlying activity.

Merchant spending ​

The Merchant spending chart ranks merchants by the total amount of money spent with them during the selected period.

Each merchant displays:

  • Its position in the spending ranking
  • The merchant name and logo, when available
  • The number of matching transactions
  • The total amount spent

The background bar represents the merchant's spending relative to the highest-spending merchant. This makes it easier to compare merchants even when their totals are very different.

The chart displays the top ten merchants by default. If more merchants are available, click Show all merchants to expand the complete ranking. Click Show top 10 to return to the shorter view.

Only outgoing reportable transactions are included. Transactions assigned to a transfer category do not appear in the ranking.

Money map ​

The Money map follows recorded income into the areas it funded.

Income flows into available money and then into your largest spending categories. If money remained after spending, it appears as Retained. If spending was greater than income, the map shows a Funding gap.

Spending outside the six largest categories is grouped under Other spending. Hover over the map to see the value represented by each flow.
