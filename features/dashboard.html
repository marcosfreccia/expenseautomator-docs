On this page

Dashboard ​

The Dashboard gives you a monthly overview of your financial activity.

It brings together your income, spending, categories, tags, budgets and goals so you can understand where your money went and what needs your attention. Use the month selector in the top right corner to review a different period.

The heading also shows how many transactions and accounts are included in the selected month.

Monthly summary ​

The summary at the top of the Dashboard displays:

  • Income: all recorded money coming in
  • Spending: recorded money going out and the percentage of income used
  • Income left: income minus spending
  • Income left (%): the share of recorded income that has not been spent

Positive income-left values are displayed in green. If spending is greater than income, the value is displayed in red.

WARNING

Income left is not your bank balance or a spending allowance. Upcoming bills and planned savings are not deducted.

Transactions that are in transfer type categories, and ignored transactions are excluded from the summary.

Uncategorized transactions ​

The Dashboard warns you when transactions still need categorizing. It shows the number of uncategorized transactions and their total value for the selected month.

Uncategorized expenses are excluded from spending, which means the displayed income-left amount may be higher than it should be.

To review them:

  1. Click Categorize now.
  2. Select a transaction from the panel.
  3. Assign its budget or category.
  4. Save the transaction.

When every transaction in the selected month is categorized, the Dashboard displays All caught up.

Spending pace ​

The spending-pace chart shows how your spending accumulates throughout the month. It includes:

  • Your cumulative recorded spending
  • An income pace reference
  • Your average daily spending
  • Projected spending for the complete month

Enable Compare with previous month to add the previous month's cumulative spending to the chart.

Hover over the chart to inspect the values for a particular day.

What stands out ​

The What stands out section highlights notable patterns in the selected month. Depending on your activity, these insights can call attention to your spending pace, remaining income or changes in category spending.

If no activity has been recorded for the month, there may not be any insights to display.

Spending distribution ​

The spending-distribution chart groups recorded spending by category.

Hover over a section of the chart to see that category's amount. The list beside the chart shows each category's:

  • Share of total spending
  • Recorded amount
  • Increase or decrease compared with the previous month

Changes smaller than 5% are not highlighted.

TIP

Categorize transactions consistently to make the spending distribution and month-to-month comparisons more accurate.

Tag analysis ​

Tag analysis groups spending using the tags assigned to your transactions. It shows up to ten tags, together with:

  • The number of matching transactions
  • The tag's share of tagged spending
  • The total amount
  • The change from the previous month

Click Browse by tag to open the Transactions page and explore the underlying activity.

Goals and budgets ​

The Dashboard shows progress for your active goals and budgets.

For each goal, you can see the amount saved, the target amount and the percentage completed. Click Manage to open the Goals page.

For each budget, you can see the amount used compared with its limit. Click Manage to review or update your budgets.

Latest activity ​

The Latest activity section lists your most recent transactions for the selected period. Each entry shows the payee, date and amount.

Click a transaction to open its details, or click All transactions to view the complete transaction list.

Import transactions ​

Click Import CSV in the top right corner to add transactions from a supported bank statement. After importing, review uncategorized transactions so the Dashboard totals and comparisons reflect the new activity.

For the complete import workflow, see Transactions.
